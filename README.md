### Batya R. Nightingale

**Molecular diagnostics, genomics, and digital pathology for wildlife health.**

I'm a bioinformatician in New York. I coordinate digital pathology at NYU Langone Health and I'm completing an M.S. in Bioinformatics at NYU. Before that I spent five years in wildlife disease diagnostics at the Wildlife Conservation Society's Bronx Zoo: multiplex qPCR assay development, pathogen screening for international field programs, novel virus characterization and environmental DNA.

My code is where the bench and the computer meet: reproducible pipelines for pathogen sequence data, built by someone who has run the assays they analyse.

#### Current work

- **[cdv-phylodynamics](https://github.com/batyanight/cdv-phylodynamics)**: Bayesian phylodynamics showing a North American canine distemper virus lineage is maintained in wild carnivores, not reseeded from dogs. [Interactive phylogeny](https://nextstrain.org/community/batyanight/cdv-phylodynamics) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22182061)
- **[wildphylo](https://github.com/batyanight/wildphylo)**: a config-driven phylodynamics pipeline for wildlife disease, with gates that stop an analysis when the data can't support it.
- **AssayWatch** *(in development)*: re-screens published PCR and qPCR assays against newly deposited sequences to flag primer and probe drift.
